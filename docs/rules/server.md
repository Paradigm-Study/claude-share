# Rules: The server

These are among the rules that shaped the design ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) lists every area and when to read it). Don't undo these without new evidence. Names in backticks are where to look.

- **The server runs on Cloudflare alone**, in TypeScript. `server/core/` is the room's logic over the Fetch API, imported through `core/index.ts`; `server/worker/` (the Workers and Durable Objects, a file each: `app.ts`, `room-object.ts`, `directory.ts`, `gate.ts`, `host-container.ts`, entered through `index.ts`) keeps the sockets, timers and storage, and `app.ts` routes the main Worker's paths with Hono, in order (what the server says of itself, the home page, traces, the directory, then a room's). Its handlers never touch Hono's `c.res`, so a room's own Response, a WebSocket's upgrade included, goes back as it is. The Node server was removed in October 2026; its tests run the Workers under `wrangler dev` (`test/support/workerd.ts`, two processes: the preview Worker reaches the main one's `RoomObject` through wrangler's dev registry, and the configs leave out the container image, which needs a Cloudflare login). `wrangler dev`'s own proxy loses an answer the Worker gives before reading a request's body: refusals by declared length are tested through `worker.fetch` directly. Every body is read through `readBody`, bounded in bytes as it arrives, and the outer Worker buffers no room body (`bodyLimit`, `bodyTooLarge`). Keep `wrangler.toml`'s `compatibility_date` no newer than the wrangler you deploy with supports.
- **Nothing runs on a timer while a room is quiet.** Who is still here is settled by the next request, or by one alarm (`schedule()`), written only when what's due is sooner than the one set. On Cloudflare the Worker, not the Durable Object, holds a client's stream (an object holding an open response is billed by the second) and talks to the room over a hibernating WebSocket; a change goes to stream sockets alone (`broadcast()`).
- **What a client holds open is bounded.** A seat (the host's too) holds `SEAT_STREAMS` (4) streams and `SEAT_POLLS` (4) long polls at once; one more ends its oldest stream, or answers its oldest poll at once (a reconnect's old stream, not noticed gone yet, is the usual one). A client reconnects from its cursor, so nothing is lost.
- **A seat let go comes back.** A guest with no connection for 45 s is let go but its token kept (`away`, the newest 100), and it's seated again as it was when next used (`comeBack`). The plugin takes a new seat on a 401 (`reseat`, `stillSeated`), and only a room that's gone (404, 410) ends its place. A Leave or the away drop takes what the seat had for previews (`revokeSeat`).
- **One person, one entry, wherever they are.** A share or a join carries the person's sign-in when there is one (`x-shared-session-account`), so the room lists a person once with all their seats (`people`, `isMe`). On one computer, a session in a room holds it in `$.store` (`holdRoom`), so a link to that room opens that session (`openHeld`) instead of joining twice.
- **Everything is bounded in bytes.** An event's strings are cut at 32,000 characters and its nesting at 32 levels (`clampBody`); a guest's event past 128 KiB is refused and a host's cut to fit (`EVENT_BYTES`, `fitBody`). A room keeps 5,000 events and 16 MiB of them (`HISTORY_LIMIT`, `HISTORY_BYTES`), 128 open previews, and 100 MB of files, each file's record kept beside its bytes (`bm:`) so `meta` stays under 1 MiB; a file going up is counted against that before it's stored (`filesPending`), so uploads at once can't pass it together, and a room reads `FILE_UPLOADS` (4) at once, 429 past it; a file going out, and a preview's answer, count against what the room may send (`SERVE_LIMITS`, in AGENTS.md's "Running the public server"). A room loaded from storage starts its seq past any it may have handed out (`Room.resume`).
- **Teams live in the server's directory** (`server/teams.ts`, `Directory`), and rooms only ask it (`shareCheck`, `joinCheck`) and tell it (`register`, `unregister`): on Cloudflare by calling the `DirectoryObject`'s methods on its stub (Durable Object RPC), which has no path for the Worker to forward. What it has timed (session invites, sign-ins under way) is deleted when due whether or not anyone asks (`sweep`, the directory object's alarm told when by `onDue`), and a room's end or expiry takes its invites and its place on its team's list (`roomEnded`, through `ended`; a session's invites are indexed by `rinv:`). Deleting an account also takes the invites it sent and its sessions' places on its teams' lists. A members-only team's rooms hear from it whom the team has now, after someone leaves or is removed and when it turns members-only (`enforce`, `keepTeam`; on Cloudflare the `RoomObject`'s `keepTeam`, called over RPC, never a path), and let every other guest seat go, here or away (`Room.keepTeam`, reason `removed`), with what it holds for previews; a stream or a long poll whose seat is gone ends (`seated`). A join asks the team after its waits (its body, the sign-in's look), and again if the team let someone go meanwhile (`teamGen`), so a removal while it's under way never ends in a seat. An account makes 30 changes a minute at most.
- **A sign-in's token never travels in an address or argv, and only the browser's Continue signs in.** The plugin opens `/auth/<provider>/start?login=<sha256 of a secret>` and collects the token by POSTing the secret to `/api/auth/poll` (`startSignIn`). Whoever makes a start link chooses its `login`, so the page asks first, showing a code the plugin shows too (`signInCode`); it's never framed, cached or sent as a referrer, runs one script allowed by its hash (`CONTINUE_SCRIPT`), and enables Continue only once it has been seen and focused, so a page raised under a double-click can't take its second click. The token is minted at the poll that collects it, and the server keeps only hashes. An account's email is proof only of what its newest sign-in verified (`emailVerified`): one the provider stops vouching for is shown still, but leaves the index (`byEmail`) and takes no team by its domain; GitHub's addresses unread fail the sign-in rather than read as none. It's saved per server in `shared-session/account.json` in the config folder (`accountFile`, `umask 077`) and sent only to the server that gave it. `AUTH_TEST=1` (`wrangler dev --var AUTH_TEST:1`) adds a test provider; never set it on a deployed Worker.
- **Traces keep a shared session's turns for its team, only where a server turns them on** (`server/traces`, TypeScript; `TRACES_DB`, a Hyperdrive binding, and `OAUTH_KV`; `tracesOn`). The Worker loads that code only then (a dynamic `import()`), so a server without them never loads it. A room hands its turns over as each one ends, after its answer (`keepSoon`, `keepNew`), one write at a time, through an outbox in its storage (`traces:*`, 500 turns at most) that an alarm tries again a minute later; the room's end keeps the open turn. Only the host's rows count, text alone: a turn is a prompt, Claude's text after it and its tools' names (`takeTurns`). It goes to its team's scope, else its signed-in host's, else nowhere (`tracesScope`), and names who asked by account only when the room can tell (`authorOf`: a name alone is never proof). Search is over MCP at `/mcp` (`@modelcontextprotocol/server`), reached by the plugin's own MCP server (`plugin/relay/traces-mcp.mts`) with the plugin's account token (`ssa_`, `accountToken`), so nobody adds it by hand; a client without the plugin reaches it by OAuth 2.1 (`@cloudflare/workers-oauth-provider`; Hono for the one page). The page signs in as `/team` does, the server collecting the account token itself, and lets only a loopback or claude.ai redirect through (`allowedRedirect`), since anyone may register a client. Every MCP call asks the directory who the grant's sign-in is now (`whoIs`), so the scopes are today's. Keywords need every word of the query (`keywordPart`); keywords and meaning are ranked together (reciprocal rank). The home page says the server keeps turns only when it does (`homePage`'s `traces`).
- **Native pool enrollment checks live membership, never a link** (`server/native-pool.ts`, `docs/native-pool.md`): exact account and team membership now, never `joinCheck` or link access. Its two named `/rpc/` fetch paths belong only to the private `DirectoryObject` cross-script binding; `Directory.owns` must never include them and the public Worker must never forward them. Its grants are hashed, verifier-bound, pool-origin-bound, single-use, capped per account and swept after two minutes. Keep their sign-in tokens, grants and verifiers out of URLs and logs.
- **Invites go to accounts** (`POST rooms/:id/invite`, kept 12 hours), fetched by one session for the whole machine (`pollInvites`, `INVITES_KEY`). **Join** joins in place only in a fresh session (`joinHere`); otherwise it opens a new Claude Desktop session with `claude://code/new?q=<link>`, or copies `claude '<link>'` in a terminal, one room at a time (`JOIN_QUEUE`).
